💡 Empirical Benchmark Insights: Why TF-IDF Won

TF-IDF Accuracy

Captures Exact Topical "Aboutness"

Rare, high-IDF keywords (alien, invasion, science fiction) trigger sharp, precise cosine activations. It successfully recommended Independence Day & Aliens.

Embedding Dilemma

The Mean-Pooling Dilution Trap

Averaging word vectors across 300+ words pulls document vectors toward a generic review centroid (scene, plot, character), causing CGI demos to outrank true plot matches.

Skip-Gram Advantage

Tone & Niche Word Sensitivity

Skip-Gram outperformed CBOW significantly on rare tokens and sentiment matching (80% tone consistency), making it the superior dense representation.

Why Do Cosine Scales Differ?

  • TF-IDF Cosine (~0.13): Documents live in a 79,696-dimensional sparse space. Since a query only contains ~25 terms, 99.9% of coordinates are zero.
  • Word2Vec Cosine (~0.91): Averaging all 330 words in each review squeezes every document vector into a narrow hyper-cone in 100-D space, creating high numerical cosine scores without indicating high confidence.
